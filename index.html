About us:

DJGUARACHA's upbringing always had a soundtrack, giving them the urge to recreate all the sounds and textures they heard in their favorite songs. Completly careless of any limitation, their taste has been esculpted by artists ranging from Los Askis, to Iglooghost, XXYYXX, Armin Van Buren, John Digweed and Big L just to mention some of them. Given they grew up somewhere in Mexico City, they felt the need to combine the rich sonidero and street mexican culture with the abstract concepts he liked from IDM and Electronic music. After a 10 year long journey, we can finally say we've reached a stage where we have achieved such combination to perfection, however, there has been many identities to our esence and there will continue to be.
