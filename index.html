About us:

Started in FL Studio circa 2016, DJGUARACHA represents the latest stage of a decade long exercise in auditory and emotional synthesis. Built on an vast range of influences such as the ambient garage of XXYYXX, the progressive architecture of Hernan Cattaneo, John Digweed, and Nick Warren, the raw rock energy of Foo Fighters and RHCP, and the sub-bass devastation of Excision. We see rigid genre labels as a mere jail, that is why, DJ GUARACHA functions as a temporary conceptual anchor: a hyper-specific identity deployed to navigate low-frequency dynamics, Sonidero chaos, and raw emotional output. Subject to continuous iteration.
