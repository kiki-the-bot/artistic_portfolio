░▒▓█[ ABOUT_ME ]█▓▒░

DJGUARACHA's upbringing always had a soundtrack, giving them the urge to recreate all the sounds and textures they heard in their favorite songs. Completly careless of any limitation, their taste has been esculpted by artists ranging from Los Askis, to Iglooghost, XXYYXX, Armin Van Buren, John Digweed and Big L just to mention some of them. Given they grew up somewhere in Mexico City, they felt the need to combine the rich sonidero and street mexican culture with the abstract visual and auditive concepts he liked from IDM and Electronic music. After a 10 year long journey, we can finally say we've reached a stage where we have achieved such combination to perfection. This is just one of the many identities we've used to express our passion for art and it won't be the last one.

░▒▓█[ LATEST_RELEASE ]█▓▒░

La verdad, agarré la inspiración para este release por mi gusto hacia lo esotérico y el redescubrimiento de aquella ola de música tribal que se dio en Monterrey en su momento. Tuve esta epifanía cuando andaba en la combi y el chofer traía unas rolas de guaracha bien pesadas, y pensé que tenía que recrear ese sonido con una calidad técnica perfecta, jajaja. Empezó como un experimento irónico en cierta parte, pero al final me di cuenta de que estaba muy bueno para dejarlo morir en la ironía.
